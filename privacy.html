1. Information We Collect

To provide high-end consulting, immigration assistance, and international recruitment services, Career Clout collects certain personal and technical data when you visit our website, submit inquiries, or consult with our advisors.

Personal Information (Voluntarily Submitted)

We collect private information you willingly provide to us when booking consultations, using our forms, or submitting files, including:

  • Full name and contact details: Enabling personalized consultation setup and administrative routing.
  • Phone number and email address: Used for direct communications, updates, and schedule notifications.
  • Country preferences and goals: For tailored visa planning, university search, and expat setups.
  • Academic and professional resumes: For headhunting and international hiring pathways.
  • Verification documents: Passports, degrees, or certifications voluntarily uploaded by you for intake review.

Technical Information (Automatically Collected)

When browsing our website, we automatically log basic technical data to ensure stability, performance, and security:

  • Device Details: Device type, browser type, operating system version, and display dimensions.
  • Network Data: Masked IP addresses, network locations, and connection speeds.
  • Usage Metrics: Time spent on pages, scroll actions, clicked buttons, and referral links.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data only for legitimate, transparent business and service delivery purposes. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Deliver personalized Student Visa, Work Permit, and Nomad Visa advisory.
  • Respond to intake requests, email inquiries, and schedule consultation blocks.
  • Coordinate document checks, academic intakes, and employer headhunting processes.
  • Send important timeline updates, invoice statements, and calendar invites.
  • Improve website design, loading speeds, mobile container rendering, and overall interface usability.
  • Secure our databases, forms, and network logs against unauthorized access or spam.

Career Clout does not perform automated profile decisions or utilize algorithmic scoring systems that affect your legal rights.

3. Information Sharing

Zero-Data-Selling Guarantee

Career Clout values your confidentiality. We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party brokers or advertisers under any circumstances.

We share information with trusted third parties strictly when necessary to facilitate the international services you have hired us to perform, including:

  • Academic Institutions: Presenting intake files to universities during admissions.
  • Hiring Employers: Submitting candidate profiles for international job opportunities.
  • Insurance Partners: Presenting application requirements for expat health insurance.
  • Sovereign Authorities: Coordination of visa and permit files with embassies and consulates when instructed.
  • Cloud Providers: Storing data in protected databases hosted by secure CRM, email, and web hosts.

4. Data Protection & Security

Career Clout deploys comprehensive technical, physical, and administrative measures to secure your personal records and files from:

  • Unauthorized back-door access or data leaks.
  • Accidental data loss, deletion, or folder damage.
  • Intellectual theft, fraudulent manipulation, or distribution.

We utilize encrypted transfer protocols (SSL/HTTPS), password-protected intake lockers, and strict employee access controls. However, you acknowledge that no online transmission mechanism or server network is 100% immune, and we cannot guarantee absolute physical safety.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies (tiny system files saved on your device) and masked analytical trackers to improve your experience on our website:

  • **Functional Cookies:** Storing layout selections, device details, and inputs to avoid double-typing.
  • **Analytical Cookies:** Evaluating page traffic trends, scroll rates, and button interaction metrics.

You have the absolute right to restrict, delete, or completely disable browser cookies through your web browser preference panels, though some dynamic components of the site may lose temporary functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website, digital guidelines, and documents may occasionally integrate or link out to external websites and systems, including payment processors, expat insurance desks, or national consular platforms.

Career Clout does not maintain, endorse, review, or control the privacy guidelines, database systems, or content of these independent third-party portals. We highly advise reading their respective privacy statements before submitting any private documents.

7. Data Retention

Career Clout stores your personal and technical details only for as long as is necessary to satisfy our consulting agreements, maintain robust corporate business files, settle invoicing procedures, or fully comply with spanish regulatory mandates.

When the retainer agreement is completed, or the mandatory record-keeping period expires, we securely destroy physical folders and permanently delete or anonymize all digital data records.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your geographic residency and region, you are entitled to several fundamental rights regarding your private data. These include the right to:

  • **Request Access:** Receive a copy of the personal records we hold for your file.
  • **Request Rectification:** Correct incomplete, wrong, or outdated details.
  • **Request Erasure:** Request the permanent deletion of your data files ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • **Withdraw Consent:** Terminate permissions given to process data channels.

To exercise any of these security rights, please contact our privacy compliance desk using the email detailed below.

9. International Data Processing

Career Clout is a global consultancy based in Barcelona, Spain. To perform our international recruitment, school placements, and immigration advisory, your personal details may be transferred across country borders to coordinates in host nations (e.g. within the European Union, Canada, or the UK).

We deploy strict technical safeguards, secure international network connections, and data transfer clauses to ensure your data stays fully protected in accordance with General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) mandates.

10. Children's Privacy

Career Clout does not knowingly structure services for, or collect personal data from, children under the age of 13 without direct parental, guardian, or legal sponsor validation.

If we discover that an underage user has submitted private data to our portals without legal parental validation, we will immediately delete that record from our databases.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Career Clout reserves the right to adjust, update, or rewrite this Privacy Policy periodically to align with evolving legal guidelines, operational shifts, or security upgrades.

Any revisions will be posted on this page with the modified "Last Updated" stamp. We encourage visiting this section occasionally to remain informed about how we safeguard your consulting data.
