1. About Career Clout

Welcome to Career Clout. We are a premier global consultancy headquartered in Barcelona, Spain. Our organization provides customized, professional support, resources, and strategic guidance for ambitious individuals navigating international relocations and global career steps.

Our core services encompass advising, administrative planning, and document navigation assistance for:

  • Student Visas: Researching universities, verifying academic pathways, and assisting with student-related consular applications.
  • Work Permits: Guiding professional work visa filings, corporate documents, and host entity coordination.
  • Immigration Assistance: Strategizing immigration streams, structural documentation review, and logistics checklists.
  • Digital Nomad Visas: Assisting independent contractors, remote employees, and entrepreneurs with visa criteria.
  • International Recruitment: Sourcing qualified global candidates and offering comprehensive career coaching.
  • Insurance Guidance: Recommending specialized international travel and expat health policies.
